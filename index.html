About this project

How it started

It always goes the same way. A song comes on, one second of it plays, and I know it — I just can't say the name. That gap between recognising something and being able to name it is the whole idea here.

Daily song games got me thinking about it, but I wanted one that wasn't a single track a day and wasn't tied to whatever's charting. So this one lets you pick any artist and works through their whole catalogue. You start with one second. Miss, and you earn two, then four, seven, eleven, sixteen. The deeper you get into a song, the more it costs you.

The artists on the front page are the ones I actually listen to, which makes it a reasonable read on how well I know my own library. Usually worse than I'd like.

Built as a single HTML file with no framework and no build step. Audio comes from Deezer's public API, which serves 30-second previews and needs no account or key. The waveform is drawn on a canvas from the decoded audio, guess matching is fuzzy so near-misses on spelling still count, and your streak is kept in local storage.
